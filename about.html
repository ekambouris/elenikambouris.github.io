Wow, this is such a cool website! How did you make it?

Well, I am a very curious individual and also a nerd. When I found out that job applications today were asking for portfolios my first thought was back to an intro computer science class I took back in high school. I wondered how much html and css I remembered and what sort of website I could create on my own. It has been a very rewarding experience and super fun to discover all sorts of design tricks to personalize the site and make it something that is very me. I try to apply this approach to everything I do in both life and work - get curious and really put 100% effort in to create a product that I am not only satisfied with, but proud of.

Is graphic design your passion?

Great question! No! I am passionate about space and advanced air mobility. I enjoy working in situations where the problem is worked from beginning to end, ideally with a physical outcome. I like the design-build-test-repeat engineering process and find it very rewarding. Being involved in aircraft development has taught me a lot about working through the unknown, making changes when ideas don’t work, and that being flexible in order to achieve the desired result is necessary. I value good teamwork and communication as I have seen how important it is to the design process and overall team dynamic. I am drawn to roles where engineering decisions have a direct impact on system performance.
