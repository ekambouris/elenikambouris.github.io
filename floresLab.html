Lab Overview

The Flores Research Group focuses on understanding the relationship between material structure and mechanical behavior for a variety of engineered and natural materials. Current research projects study bulk metallic glasses, multi-principal element alloys, and geological materials.

Role Overview

My research project focused on the Bulk Metallic Glasses (BMG) side of the group’s work. The lab had previously found success depositing dots of Vitreloy-106a onto a Zirconium substrate using direct-laser-deposition additive manufacturing. My work focused on expanding upon the dots and creating walls to check for amorphous behavior. I varied laser speed, laser power, powder feed rate, and movement rates. I learned how to work in a laboratory environment, prepare substrates, direct-laser-deposition, and polish results for microscopy to analyze results.
