Design Build Fly

Technical President

2024 - 2025

Reports!

Club Overview

Design Build Fly (DBF) is an aerospace engineering design team that constructs a remote-control (RC) aircraft for an annual flyoff competition hosted by the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA). The group is comprised of six (6) technical sub-teams: aerodynamics & payload, simulation development, structures, manufacturing, avionics, and propulsion. These teams are headed by club leadership and executives to work through the aircraft design, build, and fly process from start to finish.

Role Overview

As Technical President, I was responsible for coordinating the efforts of all six (6) technical sub-teams to streamline the process and successfully build an optimized aircraft with the best possible performance at competition. This included running weekly meetings with the leadership team to gather progress updates from individual team leads and setting expectations for work to be completed in the coming week. I managed the project scheduling to ensure everything was completed on time and made adjustments accordingly should things jump ahead or fall behind. Additionally, I controlled the direction of the aircraft configuration - what parameters were prioritized in the optimizer, and all other dimensions of the aircraft. Finally, I wrote the proposal for entrance into the competition and managed the design report process - important for flight order placement and score multiplier at the competition. In the 2024-2025 competition season, the club received a 14th-place proposal score, a 26th-place design report score, and a 35th-place competition result.

Role Overview

The DBF Manufacturing Team is responsible for building the aircraft from start to finish. We ensure a solid construction of an aircraft so that it can handle takeoff, flight, landing, and any other payloads for competition missions. The entire aircraft is built in-house, so Manufacturing Team requires knowledge and expertise in laser cutting, fiberglass layups, hot-wire foam cutting, and working with carbon fiber. For my role as a Manufacturing Team Lead, I oversaw a team of 8-10 members through the process of constructing the aircraft. I instructed members to develop proficiency in all manufacturing techniques and tools that the club uses.
