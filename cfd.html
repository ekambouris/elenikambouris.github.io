Project 1 Overview

This report examines the effects of mesh grid size on the accuracy of the Laplace Scheme of the 2D Heat Equation on a uniform unit plate. The accuracy and convergence rates are tested for 4x4, 8x8, 16x16, and 32x32 grids. These results are compared to exact solutions, truncation error, and an improved scheme.

Project 2 Overview

This report examines the accuracy of different schemes to numerically solve the linear advection equation. Specifically, Lax-Friedrichs, First-order Upwind, Lax-Wendroff, and Minimum Dispersion. Each of these schemes is used to try and match initial conditions and later, a shock condition. Additionally, an updated flux formula without scheme dependency is introduced. The schemes are tested on a row vector with M = 32 grid spacings and computed to a given time T. We calculate L1 errors, phase errors, and amplitudes at 4 different CFL numbers.

Project 3 Overview

This report examines four different schemes to solve the traffic equation. Specifically, the exact Riemann, Lax-Wendroff, Roe’s Approximation, and Roe’s Approximation with Entropy Fix. It is found that exact Riemann and Roe’s with the Entropy fix do the best job at matching the exact behavior. Lax-Wendroff shows a lot of oscillations, and regular Roe’s does nothing. All four schemes are compared with each other and at three different safety factors as the simulation steps through time.
