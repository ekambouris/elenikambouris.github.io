Class/Project Overview

In Senior Design, I developed skills in prototyping, iterating, and principle-based design. I worked with a group to help out a local business with a problem they were facing - in this case, art school professor Dr. Mary Ruppert-Stroescu. Dr. Ruppert-Stroescu runs a fashion company that makes clothing out of strips and odd shapes of recycled fabrics. To do this, she uses a water-soluble sheeting that the scraps are sewn into. Our project was to create a washing machine that could remove the sheeting efficiently without causing any warping to the clothing items.
